Approach 1: Memoization Search

Intuition

According to the problem, in a balanced permutation, the sum of the numbers at odd index positions is equal to the sum of the numbers at even index positions. For the given string num, we need to find the number of different permutations of num that are balanced permutations. Let the sum of all digits in num be tot. According to the definition of a balanced permutation, the total sum tot must be divisible by 2. This means the sum of the digits at even positions and the sum at odd positions must both equal 2tot?. If tot is odd and cannot be evenly divided, then it is impossible to form a balanced permutation, and we return 0 immediately.
