# Prefix Sum

Prefix sums are trivial to compute in Computer Science we simple set `y_i = y_(i -1) + x_i`

For example the prefix sum of the `natural numbers` is simply the `triangle numbers`.

```
1   2   3   4   5   6   ...
1   3   6   10  15  21  ...
```

or more generally known by Gauss' formula for the first `n` numbers of `(n * (n + 1)) / 2`
