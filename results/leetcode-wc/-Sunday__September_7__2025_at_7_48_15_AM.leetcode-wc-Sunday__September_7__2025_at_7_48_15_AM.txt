531. Lonely Pixel I;=;=Medium;=;=public int findLonelyPixel(char[][] picture);=;=Given an m x n picture consisting of black 'B' and white 'W' pixels, return the number of black lonely pixels.

A black lonely pixel is a character 'B' that located at a specific position where the same row and same column don't have any other black pixels.

 

Example 1:


Input: picture = [["W","W","B"],["W","B","W"],["B","W","W"]]
Output: 3
Explanation: All the three 'B's are black lonely pixels.
Example 2:


Input: picture = [["B","B","B"],["B","B","W"],["B","B","B"]]
Output: 0
 

Constraints:

m == picture.length
n == picture[i].length
1 <= m, n <= 500
