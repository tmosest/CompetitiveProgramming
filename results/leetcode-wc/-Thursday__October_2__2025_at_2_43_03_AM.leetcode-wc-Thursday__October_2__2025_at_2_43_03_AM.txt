Count Odd Numbers in an Interval Range;=;=Easy;=;=public int countOdds(int low, int high)         if (low==high) return (low%2==0)?0:1;        low=(low%2==0)?low+1:low;        high=(high%2==0)?high-1:high;        int count=(low!=high)?2:0;        while (low<high)            low+=2;            high-=2;            count=(low<high)?count+2:count;                count=(low==high)?count+1:count;        return count;;=;=Given two non-negative integers low and high. Output: 3
Explanation: The odd numbers between 3 and 7 are [3,5,7].
Example 2:

Input: low = 8, high = 10
