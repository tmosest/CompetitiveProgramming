2826. Sorting Three Groups;=;=Medium;=;=public int minimumOperations(List<Integer> nums);=;=You are given an integer array nums. Each element in nums is 1, 2 or 3. In each operation, you can remove an element from nums. Return the minimum number of operations to make nums non-decreasing.

 

Example 1:

Input: nums = [2,1,3,2,1]

Output: 3

Explanation:

One of the optimal solutions is to remove nums[0], nums[2] and nums[3].

Example 2:

Input: nums = [1,3,2,1,3,3]

Output: 2

Explanation:

One of the optimal solutions is to remove nums[1] and nums[2].

Example 3:

Input: nums = [2,2,2,2,3,3]

Output: 0

Explanation:

nums is already non-decreasing.

 

Constraints:

1 <= nums.length <= 100
1 <= nums[i] <= 3
