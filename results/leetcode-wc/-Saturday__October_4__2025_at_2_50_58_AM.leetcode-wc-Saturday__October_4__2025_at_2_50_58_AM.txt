2447. Number of Subarrays With GCD Equal to K;=;=Medium;=;=public int subarrayGCD(int[] nums, int k);=;=Given an integer array nums and an integer k, return the number of subarrays of nums where the greatest common divisor of the subarray's elements is k.

A subarray is a contiguous non-empty sequence of elements within an array.

The greatest common divisor of an array is the largest integer that evenly divides all the array elements.

 

Example 1:

Input: nums = [9,3,1,2,6,3], k = 3
Output: 4
Explanation: The subarrays of nums where 3 is the greatest common divisor of all the subarray's elements are:
- [9,3,1,2,6,3]
- [9,3,1,2,6,3]
- [9,3,1,2,6,3]
- [9,3,1,2,6,3]
Example 2:

Input: nums = [4], k = 7
Output: 0
Explanation: There are no subarrays of nums where 7 is the greatest common divisor of all the subarray's elements.
