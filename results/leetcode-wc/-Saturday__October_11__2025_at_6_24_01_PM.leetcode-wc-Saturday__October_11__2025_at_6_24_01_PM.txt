Example 1:



Input: n = 2, m = 3
Output: 3
Explanation: 3 squares are necessary to cover the rectangle.
2 (squares of 1x1)
1 (square of 2x2)
Example 2:



Input: n = 5, m = 8
Output: 5

Input: n = 11, m = 13
Output: 6
