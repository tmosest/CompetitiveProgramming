829. Consecutive Numbers Sum;=;=Hard;=;=public int consecutiveNumbersSum(int n);=;=Given an integer n, return the number of ways you can write n as the sum of consecutive positive integers.

 

Example 1:

Input: n = 5
Output: 2
Explanation: 5 = 2 + 3
Example 2:

Input: n = 9
Output: 3
Explanation: 9 = 4 + 5 = 2 + 3 + 4
Example 3:

Input: n = 15
Output: 4
Explanation: 15 = 8 + 7 = 4 + 5 + 6 = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5
 

Constraints:
