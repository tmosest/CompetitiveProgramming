1044. Longest Duplicate Substring;=;=Hard;=;=public String longestDupSubstring(String s);=;=Given a string s, consider all duplicated substrings: (contiguous) substrings of s that occur 2 or more times. The occurrences may overlap.

Return any duplicated substring that has the longest possible length. If s does not have a duplicated substring, the answer is "".

 

Example 1:

Input: s = "banana"
Output: "ana"
Example 2:

Input: s = "abcd"
Output: ""
 

Constraints:

2 <= s.length <= 3 * 104
s consists of lowercase English
