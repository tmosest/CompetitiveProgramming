3678. Smallest Absent Positive Greater Than Average;=;=Easy;=;=public int smallestAbsent(int[] nums);=;=You are given an integer array nums.

Return the smallest absent positive integer in nums such that it is strictly greater than the average of all elements in nums.

The average of an array is defined as the sum of all its elements divided by the number of elements.
 

Example 1:

Input: nums = [3,5]

Output: 6

Explanation:

The average of nums is (3 + 5) / 2 = 8 / 2 = 4.
The smallest absent positive integer greater than 4 is 6.
