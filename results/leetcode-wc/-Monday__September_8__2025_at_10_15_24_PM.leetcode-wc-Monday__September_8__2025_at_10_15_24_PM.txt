1259. Handshakes That Don't Cross;=;=Hard;=;=public int numberOfWays(int numPeople);=;=You are given an even number of people numPeople that stand around a circle and each person shakes hands with someone else so that there are numPeople / 2 handshakes total.

Return the number of ways these handshakes could occur such that none of the handshakes cross.

Since the answer could be very large, return it modulo 109 + 7.

 

Example 1:


Input: numPeople = 4
Output: 2
Explanation: There are two ways to do it, the first way is [(1,2),(3,4)] and the second one is [(2,3),(4,1)].
Example 2:


Input: numPeople = 6
Output: 5
 

Constraints:

2 <= numPeople <= 1000
