1447. Simplified Fractions;=;=Medium;=;=public List<String> simplifiedFractions(int n);=;=Given an integer n, return a list of all simplified fractions between 0 and 1 (exclusive) such that the denominator is less-than-or-equal-to n. You can return the answer in any order.

 

Example 1:

Input: n = 2
Output: ["1/2"]
Explanation: "1/2" is the only unique fraction with a denominator less-than-or-equal-to 2.
Example 2:

Input: n = 3
Output: ["1/2","1/3","2/3"]
Example 3:

Input: n = 4
Output: ["1/2","1/3","1/4","2/3","3/4"]
Explanation: "2/4" is not a simplified fraction because it can be simplified to "1/2".
