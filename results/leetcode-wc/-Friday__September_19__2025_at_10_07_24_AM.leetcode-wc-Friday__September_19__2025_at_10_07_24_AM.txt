3490. Count Beautiful Numbers;=;=Hard;=;=public int beautifulNumbers(int l, int r);=;=You are given two positive integers, l and r. A positive integer is called beautiful if the product of its digits is divisible by the sum of its digits.

Return the count of beautiful numbers between l and r, inclusive.

 

Example 1:

Input: l = 10, r = 20

Output: 2

Explanation:

The beautiful numbers in the range are 10 and 20.

Example 2:

Input: l = 1, r = 15

Output: 10

Explanation:

The beautiful numbers in the range are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10.
