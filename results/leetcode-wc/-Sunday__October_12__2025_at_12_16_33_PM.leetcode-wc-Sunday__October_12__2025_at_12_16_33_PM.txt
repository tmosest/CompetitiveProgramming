Minimum Possible Integer After at Most K Adjacent Swaps On Digits;=;=Hard;=;=public String minInteger(String num, int k);=;=You are given a string num representing the digits of a very large integer and an integer k. You are allowed to swap any two adjacent digits of the integer at most k times.

Return the minimum integer you can obtain also as a string.

 

Example 1:


Input: num = "4321", k = 4
Output: "1342"
Explanation: The steps to obtain the minimum integer from 4321 with 4 adjacent swaps are shown.
Example 2:

Input: num = "100", k = 1
Output: "010"
Explanation: It's ok for the output to have leading zeros, but the input is guaranteed not to have any leading zeros.
Example 3:

Input: num = "36789", k = 1000
Output: "36789"
Explanation: We can keep the number without any swaps.
 

Constraints:

1 <= num.length <= 3 * 104
num consists of only digits and does not contain leading zeros.
