OPTIMAL APPROACH ALGORITHM:

1. Prioritize the Higher-Value Substring

Let:

  • high=max(x,y)
  • low=min(x,y)

We prioritize removing the substring corresponding to the higher score:

  • If x³y: process ÔÔab" first
  • If y>x: process ÔÔba" first

This is implemented by setting:

  • ch1?ÔaÕ, ch2?ÔbÕ if x³y
  • ch1?ÔbÕ, ch2?ÔaÕ if y>x

We ensure that we always process ch1+ch2 as the high-value substring.

2. Remove High-Value Substrings

Traverse the string left to right, simulating a stack using counters:

  • Maintain a count of unmatched ch1 characters
  • For each ch2 encountered:
    • If there is unmatched ch1, pair them and increment score by high
    • Otherwise, store the unmatched ch2

When a non-relevant character is encountered, compute:

score increment=min(unmatched ch1, unmatched ch2)álow?

Reset both counters and continue.

3. Final Segment Residuals

After the loop, there may still be unmatched ch1 and ch2 remaining.

Add their contribution:

score increment=min(unmatched ch1, unmatched ch2)álow?

Compute Total Score

The total score is the sum of:

  • all high-value substring matches encountered directly, and
  • all residual low-value substring matches formed from remaining unpaired characters.

Let's recall all the variables:

  • primary=max(x,y)
  • secondary=min(x,y)
  • cnt1 Ñ primary counter = total number of removed high-value substrings.
  • cnt2 Ñ secondary counter = total number of removed low-value substrings from residual characters

Then the total score is:

totalScore=(cnt1*primary)+(cnt2*secondary)??

Where:

  • cnt1 is incremented directly during traversal
  • cnt2=min(unmatched ch1, unmatched ch2) in each isolated character segment and after the final segment
  • Time Complexity: O(n)
  • Space Complexity: O(1) on most language
