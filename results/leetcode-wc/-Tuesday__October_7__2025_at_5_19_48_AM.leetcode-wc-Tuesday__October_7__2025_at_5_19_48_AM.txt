248. Strobogrammatic Number III;=;=Hard;=;=public int strobogrammaticInRange(String low, String high);=;=Given two strings low and high that represent two integers low and high where low <= high, return the number of strobogrammatic numbers in the range [low, high].

A strobogrammatic number is a number that looks the same when rotated 180 degrees (looked at upside down).

 

Example 1:

Input: low = "50", high = "100"
Output: 3
Example 2:

Input: low = "0", high = "0"
Output: 1
 

Constraints:

1 <= low.length, high.length <= 15
low and high consist of only digits.
low <= high
