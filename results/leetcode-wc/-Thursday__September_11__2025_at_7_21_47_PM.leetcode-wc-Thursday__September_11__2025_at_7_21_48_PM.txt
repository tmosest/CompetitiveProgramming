1344. Angle Between Hands of a Clock;=;=Medium;=;=public double angleClock(int hour, int minutes);=;=Given two numbers, hour and minutes, return the smaller angle (in degrees) formed between the hour and the minute hand.

Answers within 10-5 of the actual value will be accepted as correct.

 

Example 1:


Input: hour = 12, minutes = 30
Output: 165
Example 2:


Input: hour = 3, minutes = 30
Output: 75
Example 3:


Input: hour = 3, minutes = 15
Output: 7.5
 

Constraints:

1 <= hour <= 12
