Recover Binary Search Tree;=;=Medium;=;=/ÊÊDefinitionÊforÊaÊbinaryÊtreeÊnode.ÊÊpublicÊÊTreeNodeÊÊÊÊÊÊÊintÊval;ÊÊÊÊÊÊTreeNodeÊleft;ÊÊÊÊÊÊTreeNodeÊright;ÊÊÊÊÊÊTreeNode()ÊÊÊÊÊÊÊTreeNode(intÊval)ÊÊthis.valÊ=Êval;ÊÊÊÊÊÊÊTreeNode(intÊval,ÊTreeNodeÊleft,ÊTreeNodeÊright)ÊÊÊÊÊÊÊÊÊÊÊthis.valÊ=Êval;ÊÊÊÊÊÊÊÊÊÊthis.leftÊ=Êleft;ÊÊÊÊÊÊÊÊÊÊthis.rightÊ=Êright;;=;=You are given the root of a binary search tree (BST), where the values of exactly two nodes of the tree were swapped by mistake. Recover the tree without changing its structure.

Ê

Example 1:


Input: root = [1,3,null,null,2]
Output: [3,1,null,null,2]
Explanation: 3 cannot be a left child of 1 because 3 > 1. Swapping 1 and 3 makes the BST valid.
Example 2:


Input: root = [3,1,4,null,null,2]
Output: [2,1,4,null,null,3]
Explanation: 2 cannot be in the right subtree of 3 because 2 < 3. Swapping 2 and 3 makes the BST valid.
Ê

Constraints:

The number of nodes in the tree is in the range [2, 1000].
-231 <= Node.val <= 231 - 1
Ê
