491. Non-decreasing Subsequences;=;=Medium;=;=public List<List<Integer>> findSubsequences(int[] nums);=;=Given an integer array nums, return all the different possible non-decreasing subsequences of the given array with at least two elements. You may return the answer in any order.

 

Example 1:

Input: nums = [4,6,7,7]
Output: [[4,6],[4,6,7],[4,6,7,7],[4,7],[4,7,7],[6,7],[6,7,7],[7,7]]
Example 2:

Input: nums = [4,4,3,2,1]
Output: [[4,4]]
 

Constraints:

1 <= nums.length <= 15
