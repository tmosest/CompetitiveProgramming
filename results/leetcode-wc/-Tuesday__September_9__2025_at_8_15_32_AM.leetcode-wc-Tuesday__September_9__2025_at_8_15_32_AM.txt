311. Sparse Matrix Multiplication;=;=Medium;=;=public int[][] multiply(int[][] mat1, int[][] mat2);=;=Given two sparse matrices mat1 of size m x k and mat2 of size k x n, return the result of mat1 x mat2. You may assume that multiplication is always possible.

 

Example 1:


Input: mat1 = [[1,0,0],[-1,0,3]], mat2 = [[7,0,0],[0,0,0],[0,0,1]]
Output: [[7,0,0],[-7,0,3]]
Example 2:

Input: mat1 = [[0]], mat2 = [[0]]
Output: [[0]]
 

Constraints:

m == mat1.length
k == mat1[i].length == mat2.length
n == mat2[i].length
1 <= m, n, k <= 100
